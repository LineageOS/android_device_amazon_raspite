Device configuration for Amazon Fire HD 8 / HD 8 Plus (12th Gen)
================================================================

The Amazon Fire HD 8 (2022), Fire HD 8 Plus (2022) and Fire HD 8 (2024)
(all codenamed _"raspite"_) are tablets from Amazon.

The Fire HD 8 and Fire HD 8 Plus were released in 2022, and a refreshed
Fire HD 8 followed in 2024.
